Flight Course Academy

Ground school to IFR for simulator pilots

16 modules3 practice gamesLive on Vercel

A full training website that takes you from a cold, dark cockpit to an instrument approach: structured modules, checkride-style quizzes, a cockpit explorer, printable checklists, a glossary, ranks and badges, and three built-in games — Flare Trainer, Radio Builder, and Pattern Perfect.

First modules are free. The point is not a video playlist — it is a course you can actually study against.

Featured project 03

Odin

Personal AI Automation Assistant

~11,700 lines5 sub-agentsVoice-controlled
Testing paused — waiting on a better machine

A voice-controlled desktop assistant with five specialized sub-agents, wake-word activation, and persistent memory across sessions. It evolved through renamed iterations — Jarvis → Kyros → Odin.

A built-in safeguard requires human approval before anything auto-publishes or sends. Next step is routing each sub-agent through a different model so they actually reason differently, not just sound different.

That testing round is on hold. I am waiting on a new laptop; the one I have starts falling over when I push the heavier model work. Design and planning are still moving. Hands-on testing starts again when the hardware can keep up.

Essam's role: Conceived, directed, and extensively tested using AI-assisted development tools. His work centered on system design and feature direction, hands-on testing and debugging — including diagnosing voice recognition and latency issues — and iterative refinement. He did not hand-write all the code himself.
Featured project 04

Vanguard Clash

Browser-based hero shooter

~10,000 linesthree.js7 heroes

A browser-based hero shooter with seven original heroes across three roles, destructible arenas, multiple objective modes, and full bot opponents. All models, textures, audio, and music are generated procedurally at runtime — nothing to download.

Its modular architecture is organized across UI, world, game, and core.

Essam's role: Hero designs, abilities, and modes were shaped through a mix of his personal creative direction and AI suggestion. The majority of implementation used AI-assisted development tools. He personally tested approximately 60% using custom smoke tests, performance tests, leak detection, and other diagnostic scripts.

Academic work beyond class

What happened

I put five people together who I had only just met. We sat with mathematics and physics almost every night for about a month before exams. I was not a teacher. I was the person who kept showing up, and who would not let the group drift because nobody wanted to be the one who asked the next question.

Alongside that

Four years as Science Fair Group Leader — same instinct, longer arc. Each year I made the project harder on purpose, from a chemistry demonstration to a live-API weather program to nuclear fission. That work is unpacked in the section below.

03 / 06

Four consecutive years · 2022–2025

Science Fair Group Leader.

2022

First time running a team through a real experiment.

The work

A volcano is a cliché and I knew that even then. The point was getting a group of people to finish something together, measure it, and stand in a hall and explain it without hiding behind the poster.

2023

Research: how the systems work, not just what they can do.

The work

I wanted to understand AI as a system, not as a magic box. We looked at how models take input, find patterns, and produce output, then tried to explain that in language a school hall would actually sit through.

2024

A program pulling live weather data for any city.

The work

First year the science fair work felt like engineering instead of a poster. We wrote a program that pulls live weather data through an API and breaks it down for any city or country you give it.

2025

Physics that does not let you stay on the surface.

The work

Chain reactions, atomic fission, and the Demon Core accident. I picked it because it would not let me stay shallow. We had to understand the physics and also what happens when people get careless with something that does not forgive mistakes.
